We keep this simple: the site works without tracking, and we do not use advertising cookies.

## What we store

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Cookie, essential | Remembers the language you chose (English or Greek). | Until you close the browser, or up to one year |
| `ca-consent` | Local storage, essential | Remembers your answer to the cookie banner, so we do not ask again. | Until you clear it |

## Payment pages

Only on the pages where you pay (checkout and managing a booking) we load **Stripe**, our payment provider. Stripe sets cookies such as `__stripe_mid` and `__stripe_sid` to prevent fraud and keep the payment secure. They are strictly necessary for paying online, so they do not need your consent, and they are not used for advertising. Details: [stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal](https://stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal).

## Visit statistics

If you choose **Allow analytics**, we count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It does not set cookies and does not identify you; we only see aggregated numbers such as page views and countries. If you choose **Only essential**, it is not loaded at all.

## Fonts, photos and video

All fonts, photos and videos are served from our own site. Nothing is loaded from Google or other third parties while you browse; the only exception is Stripe on the payment pages, as described above.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
