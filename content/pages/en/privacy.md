This policy explains what personal data we collect through {{domain}}, why, and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who we are

The data controller is **{{operator}}**, operator of Cape Avra, {{address}}.
You can reach us about anything in this policy at **{{email}}**.

## What we collect

**Early booking requests and messages.** When you use the booking request or contact form we receive your name, email address, and, if you give them, your phone number, preferred dates, number of guests and your message. If you tick the box for news about Cape Avra, we also record that consent.

**Technical data.** Like every website, our hosting provider processes the IP address and browser information needed to deliver pages and protect the site against abuse. These logs are kept for a short time.

**Visit statistics, only with your consent.** If you allow it in the cookie banner, we count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not build a profile of you and only shows us aggregated numbers.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- To answer your request and prepare a possible booking: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- To send you occasional news about Cape Avra: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
- To keep the site secure and prevent abuse: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- Once you book, to manage your stay and meet our legal and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).

## Who processes data for us

We use a small number of providers who process data only on our instructions:

- **Vercel Inc.** hosts the website.
- **Supabase** stores booking requests and messages, in a database located in the EU.
- **Resend** delivers our emails.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

Booking requests and messages that do not lead to a stay are deleted after 24 months at the latest. Booking records are kept for as long as Greek tax law requires. Your consent to receive news lasts until you withdraw it.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, a portable copy, or object to processing, by writing to {{email}}. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), [www.dpa.gr](https://www.dpa.gr).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
