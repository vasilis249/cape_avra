## Early booking requests

Before online booking opens, you can send an **early booking request** for your preferred dates.

- A request is free and without obligation, for you and for us.
- No payment is taken for a request.
- We reply personally with availability and the price for your stay. A booking only exists once we have both confirmed it in writing.

## Online bookings

When online booking is open, these terms apply to every booking made on this website. They are shown at checkout and confirmed by email, so you always know which version applies to your booking.

**Price.** The price shown at checkout includes the nightly rate, any extra-guest charge, the final cleaning and the climate resilience fee charged per night by the Greek state.

**Payment.** You pay a deposit of {{depositPercent}}% when you book. The balance is charged automatically to the same card {{balanceDays}} days before arrival; we email you if the charge does not go through, with a link to pay online. If you book less than {{balanceDays}} days before arrival, you pay the full amount at once. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.

**Security deposit.** On the day of arrival we authorise {{securityDeposit}} on your card. This is a hold, not a charge, and it is released {{releaseDays}} days after departure. We only keep part or all of it to cover damage beyond normal use, and we always contact you first.

**Cancellation by you.** You can cancel online from the link in your confirmation email, until the day before arrival:

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 working days. You can always write to us before cancelling: we may be able to move your dates instead.

**Cancellation by us.** If we have to cancel, for example because the house cannot be used, you receive a full refund of everything you have paid.
