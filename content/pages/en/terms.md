## Early booking requests

Online booking for Cape Avra is not open yet. Until it is, you can send an **early booking request** for your preferred dates.

- A request is free and without obligation, for you and for us.
- No payment is taken through this website at this stage.
- We reply personally with availability and the price for your stay. A booking only exists once we have both confirmed it in writing.

## When online booking opens

Before you can pay for a stay, this page will set out in full:

- the payment schedule (for example, a deposit at booking and the balance before arrival),
- the security deposit, if any,
- the cancellation policy and refund amounts for each notice period,
- what is included in the price, and the per-night climate resilience fee charged by the Greek state.

These terms will also be shown at checkout and confirmed by email, so you always know which version applies to your booking.
