Location & experiences

On the cape of Mytikas

Five kilometres from Preveza, on the open Ionian coast. Long beaches, ancient Nikopolis, Lefkada and Parga are all within an easy drive.

Beaches

  • The beach below the house

    Through the garden gate and a few steps down. Like every beach in Greece it is public, and outside high summer it is usually quiet.

  • Monolithi

    A long sandy beach that runs north from Mytikas for kilometres, with clear water and room for everyone.

  • Lefkada's west coast

    Porto Katsiki, Egremni and Kathisma: white cliffs and turquoise water. A full day out.

  • Parga

    Valtos and Lichnos, below the Venetian castle.

Days out

  • Preveza

    The old town and the waterfront with its tavernas, ten minutes away. Lively on summer evenings.

  • Ancient Nikopolis

    The city Octavian founded after the Battle of Actium: walls, mosaics, a theatre and a museum.

  • Lefkada

    An island you can drive to over a short bridge, with some of the best-known beaches in the Ionian.

  • Parga

    A colourful harbour town under a Venetian castle, with beaches and boat trips.

  • The Ambracian Gulf

    A calm inland sea of lagoons and wetlands, known for its dolphins and birdlife.

  • The Acheron river

    The river of myth. Walk or wade through the gorge at its springs near Glyki.

Where to eat

We are putting together our own list of favourite tavernas, and we share it with every guest.

Getting here

  • By air: Aktion airport (PVK) is about 15 minutes away, with summer flights from many European cities.
  • By car: about 4 hours from Athens on the Ionia Odos motorway.
  • A car is the easiest way to explore the area.
